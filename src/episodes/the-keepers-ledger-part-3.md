The new entry was there when she came down from the lamp on Thursday night.

It was not in Elias's hand. The letters leaned forward as if in a hurry, and the ink was still tacky when she touched it.

*Don't trust the path. Don't trust the boat. Trust the light.*

She sat with the book on her knees until the sky went pale. Out on the water, something that was not the supply boat was making for the jetty.
