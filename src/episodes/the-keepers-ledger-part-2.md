She did not sleep well. The cottage made the noises old houses make, and every one of them sounded like a footstep on the tower stairs.

In the morning she read the logbook from the beginning. Most of it was ordinary: weather, fuel, a broken pane replaced in March. The keeper's name was Elias Penhallow, and for eleven years he had written almost nothing about himself.

Then, in the last month, the entries changed.

*She will come on a Friday,* he had written in August. *She will carry the crates herself because she does not trust the path. She will be right not to.*

Mara put the book down and went to look at the path.
